THE STORY BEHIND THE MUSIC

About the Composer

Music is more than sound. It is a way to tell the stories that words cannot.

Thomas Lehmann is a composer and music-video creator based in Oregon. His work brings together expressive piano melodies, orchestral arrangements, and cinematic storytelling — music inspired by nature, memory, and the moments that stay with us.

Originally from Germany, Thomas has continued his creative journey in Oregon, composing original music and creating visual stories that give each piece its own atmosphere and emotion.

Alongside his own compositions and films, he also produces music videos for other artists, bringing their musical worlds to life through carefully crafted imagery.

“Music That Tells a Story.”
